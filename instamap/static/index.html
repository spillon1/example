Add 0 places to Google Maps

Google doesn't let other apps write to your saved places, so pick one of these (both free):

  1. One by one into a Saved list. Opens each place in Google Maps; tap Save, pick a list (e.g. "Want to go"), then come back for the next one.
  2. All at once as a map. Download the file, go to Google My Maps, Create a new map → Import, choose the file. It appears in the Google Maps app under You → Saved → Maps.

Places you open from here are marked "Saved to Google" so you can track what's done.
